Privacy & Google account access

Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

This policy describes the YouTube creator workflow of Openclaw, privately operated by Iman Affandi. It covers the local creator tools and this informational website.

Data accessed and its purpose

After a channel owner authorizes Google access, the tools use OAuth access and refresh tokens to communicate with Google. YouTube channel identifiers, titles, handles, and other channel information returned by the authorized API request are used to confirm channel identity. Videos selected by the operator, together with titles, descriptions, tags, and visibility settings, are sent to YouTube to perform the requested upload. Upload results, including video IDs, may be saved in the local production package.

Where separately authorized and used, creator reporting tools may read channel or video performance information to help the operator assess their own content. The permissions on Google's consent screen determine the access granted to a particular connection. The Ruang Kenapa longform connection requests YouTube upload and read-only access; it does not request a YouTube Analytics permission.

Storage and protection

In the local creator workflow, credentials and channel bindings are stored on the operator's computer. Longform credentials are kept in a separate local authentication directory excluded from source-control commits. The workflow checks the authorized channel before uploading. Tokens must be treated as secrets and are not published on this website. This local storage is not represented as an encrypted credential vault.

Video packages and operational records may be kept on the operator's computer. Access is limited by the computer's account and file permissions. Google handles authorization and API services under its own policies; the tools do not request or store Google passwords.

Sharing and permitted use

Selected videos and metadata are transmitted to Google/YouTube to carry out the operator's instructions. Google account data obtained through these creator connections is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train generalized AI models. Content generation services are separate from Google authorization; authorizing YouTube does not itself send Google account data to an image or voice generation service.

Openclaw creator tools' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention, disconnection, and deletion

Local credentials are retained while the operator needs the authorized connection, until removed or replaced. Revoking access prevents continued API use but does not automatically erase local files or remove videos already uploaded to YouTube. Local production records remain until the operator deletes them.

You can revoke the app's access through Google Account third-party connections. To request removal of locally stored credentials or channel records, contact imankerx@gmail.com and identify the channel concerned. The operator may verify that you control the channel before acting on a request. Uploaded videos are managed separately in YouTube Studio.

This website

These informational pages have no sign-in form, embedded media, analytics scripts, or application cookies. If hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub processes hosting requests and logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Contact and updates

Questions or privacy requests: imankerx@gmail.com. This page will be updated if the creator workflow's data handling changes.
