Openclaw creator tools help the operator prepare video packages, check the connected YouTube channel, and upload approved videos through the YouTube API.

How your data is used ↗

Privately operated by Iman Affandi. Access is intended for the operator and explicitly authorized channel owners.

01 / Prepare

Package the video

Prepare the video file, title, description, tags, and chosen visibility in the local production workflow.

02 / Connect

Confirm the channel

Google authorization connects the selected account. The longform workflow checks the channel identity before an upload.

03 / Review

Upload for review

Uploads default to private in the longform workflow, allowing review before a separate decision to publish.

Google authorization

Permission with a purpose.

YouTube upload access allows the operator to send a selected video and its metadata to YouTube. Read access allows the workflow to identify the connected channel and avoid uploading to the wrong one. Some creator tools may request additional read access for channel performance reporting; the Google consent screen shows the actual permissions requested.

Authorization takes place on Google's own pages. This website does not collect your Google password or offer a public sign-up service.
